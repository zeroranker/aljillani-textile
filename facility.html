Remote verification

How to check us without travelling

Most first orders between a buyer and a Pakistani processing unit are placed without a visit. That is normal, and it should not mean taking the supplier on trust. Here is what we will do, and what it costs you.

  1. A live video walk, not a recorded tour

    Call us and walk the floor on video, in the dye house, with the machines running. A recorded tour is staged. A live walk is not. We will put a phone in someone's hand and follow your questions.

  2. Nameplates on demand

    Ask for a photo of any machine on our list, and you will get one with its nameplate visible. If we cannot produce it, that machine is not on the floor, and we would rather find that out now than you find out at audit.

  3. A free shade-matching trial

    The cheapest and strongest test available: send us a shade you have already approved elsewhere and a piece of your fabric. We match it. A unit confident in its own lab says yes without hesitating.

    This is the test worth running before any payment

  4. Certificate verification by you, not by us

    Any certificate number we publish can be checked directly with the issuing body. We will give you the number and the expiry and let you make the call yourself. If we are not comfortable with that, you should not be either.

  5. A third-party inspection before the second order

    For a first small order, a live walk plus a free lab dip is proportionate. Before scaling up, book an independent inspection. EDIT: which third-party inspection firms do buyers usually use on Pakistan textile?
