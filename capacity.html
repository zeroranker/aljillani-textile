Where these industry ranges come from

The dyeing throughput band is anchored on an audit of 21 operating Pakistani processing mills, which found daily production spanning 4,267 to 38,427 kg/day, with a dense mid-cluster between roughly 8,600 and 26,500 kg/day. Named Faisalabad units publish figures at both ends of the range, from about 6,000 kg/day at the smaller end to 25,000 kg/day at the larger. The band above is the honest mid-sized envelope, not a marketing average.

Why we publish the benchmarks even where we have nothing to claim

Measured across 21 operating Pakistani processing mills, water consumption ran from 74 to 313 litres per kilogram, and only two of those mills were in the water-efficient band. The industry-wide average is worse. A unit that publishes its own number against a public benchmark is making a checkable claim. A unit that publishes "eco-friendly" is making none at all.
