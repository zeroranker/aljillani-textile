About the unit

A short page, deliberately. What we are, what we are not, where we are, and who you will actually be dealing with.

The company

Al-Jilanee Textile Industry (Pvt) Ltd

To confirm: company history

We are a pure textile processing unit based in area, Faisalabad, a member of the All Pakistan Textile Processing Mills Association (APTPMA), Faisalabad. The business takes fabric from buyers, processes it, dyeing, bleaching, printing and finishing, and returns it to a specification the buyer has approved.

That is the whole of the model. We do not spin yarn and we do not weave cloth. Yarn and greige are our raw materials, not our products. This matters commercially: a processing unit and a composite mill are not selling the same thing, and a quote from one is not directly comparable to a quote from the other. If you are benchmarking us against a mill quote, tell us and we will explain the difference rather than quietly matching a number we cannot meet.

Location

Faisalabad, and the processing belt we sit in

Faisalabad is one of Pakistan's largest textile clusters, with roughly 56,000 power looms and 137 spinning units recorded in the district. The processing segment around it is large, fragmented and mostly made up of small and mid-sized units, which is a good thing for a buyer who wants to talk to a decision-maker rather than a procurement department.

The Punjab government's Faisalabad district profile records around 250 textile dyeing and processing units in the district, alongside roughly 56,000 power looms and 137 spinning units. Nationally there are about 1,545 processing units, the large majority of them in Punjab. A national SME development body describes the Faisalabad weaving cluster as being supported downstream by around 210 fabric processing units and 700 cloth exporters.

What that means practically is that the skill base, the machinery service network, the spare parts supply and the trained stenter and dyehouse operators are all here. It also means most of our competitors are in the same position we are, and very few of them publish what they actually run.

Power and continuity

Anyone working in this sector knows that Faisalabad's supply has been under pressure for several years. Around 1,000 power loom units in the city closed in September 2024 citing energy shortages and tariffs, and load shedding is scheduled feeder by feeder. This is industry context rather than a specific condition at our plant, but it is the kind of thing a buyer should ask about directly, along with what our contingency is.

Working together

How we would rather work with you

Four commitments. They are ordinary, and almost nobody in this industry puts them in writing.

01

One counterparty

You deal with us, not with a chain of sub-contractors. If a lot needs to be split across two units, we coordinate it and you still have one telephone number.

02

Decisions, not silences

We would rather tell you a lot is going to be late while there is still something you can do about it than after it has shipped.

03

Your standard, not ours

Shades are matched against the standard you approve and retained against it afterwards. Our reference is yours.

04

Inspect before dispatch

Bulk does not leave without inspection against the approved sample, and we will send the result whether it is good news or not.
